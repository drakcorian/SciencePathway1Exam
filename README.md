# Year 10 Science Exam Revision

Multiple-choice revision quiz for Year 10 Science, Semester 2 exam (Willetton SHS, Task 10). Four subjects, switched with the tabs at the top. Each subject has its own levels.

**🧬 Biology** (65 questions)
1. Genetics & Crosses
2. Pedigrees & Inheritance
3. Natural Selection & Speciation
4. Evidence for Evolution
5. Exam-Style Data
6. Terminology

**🧪 Chemistry** (71)
1. Reaction Rates
2. Collision Theory & Energy
3. Particle Model
4. Acids, Bases & Reactions
5. Names, Formulas & Atoms
6. Terminology

**⚡ Physics** (89)
1. Motion Basics
2. Motion Equations & Graphs
3. Forces & Newton's Laws
4. Car Safety & Energy Transfer
5. Energy, Work & Power
6. Terminology

**🔬 Science Inquiry** (64)
1. Hypotheses & Variables
2. Fair Tests & Reliability
3. Tables & Graphs
4. Analysing Data & Conclusions
5. Terminology

Questions you get wrong come back at the end of each level until you get them right.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) and use the link on a phone.

Add `#bio`, `#chem`, `#phys` or `#inq` to the end of the link to open straight into a subject.

The **✏ Teacher** button opens an editor for the current subject's questions. Edits are saved in that browser only; **Restore defaults** brings the original set back.

## Notes

- Topics follow the 2026 Year 10 Physical Sciences course outline (Semester 2), the 2019 practice exam, and the class Science Inquiry revision booklets.
- Physics uses g = 9.8 m/s². Every calculation answer has been checked.
- Every answer equation in Chemistry has been checked to make sure the atoms balance.
- The class Science Inquiry answer key gives the mode of 3, 8, 5, 2, 5, 9, 1, 3, 5, 9 as 9. It's 5. This quiz uses the correct answer.
- Questions are new, so they don't repeat the ChemReactions, AcidsPH or Evolution quizzes.
